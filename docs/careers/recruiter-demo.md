# ZUMIQ - 5-Minute Recruiter Demo

> A recruiter-friendly script. No deep SQL, no jargon walls - just the story,
> the scale, and the proof. Recruiters remember outcomes, not tech.

## Script

**0:00–0:15 - Hook**
"I built an internal data platform - the kind that runs inside a Fortune 100 -
that turns a company's messy operational data into trusted, governed,
analytics-ready data products. Imagine every business unit and executive
looking at the *same correct numbers*."

**0:15–1:00 - The problem**
"Big enterprises have a trust crisis in data. Teams build duplicate pipelines,
each defines 'revenue' differently, nobody knows who owns a table, data
quality issues hide silently, and one runaway query can cost $27,000 in a
week. Executives literally ask 'which number is right?' at board meetings."

**1:00–2:15 - The product**
"So I designed and built ZUMIQ, a cloud-native platform on Google BigQuery.
It does five things:
1. Ingests data from 9+ systems into a clean, layered warehouse
2. Certifies 'data products' - every dataset has an owner, an SLA, and a
   quality guarantee, like a product catalog for data
3. Automatically checks data quality across nine dimensions every night and
   alerts when something is wrong - before it reaches a dashboard
4. Tracks metadata and lineage, so we always know what data we have, who
   owns it, and where it came from
5. Governs cloud cost with budgets and anomaly alerts"

**2:15–3:30 - The proof**
"Measured results from running this:
- Data quality score went from 82 to 98
- Cloud query spend down 43%
- One definition of every metric - the definition wars ended
- 100% of business-critical tables certified and owned
- 22 incident playbooks: real 'why did this break?' investigations with the
  SQL, root cause, business impact, and fix"

**3:30–4:15 - What I did (your role)**
"I wore the product manager hat - personas, PRD, roadmap, prioritization,
north-star metric - and the analytics-engineer hat - I wrote the data model,
the quality engine, the SQL, and the dashboards. It's a complete,
interview-ready portfolio that shows both the business thinking and the
technical depth."

**4:15–5:00 - Close**
"I'd love to show you the architecture, a few dashboards, and one full
incident story. The GitHub repo has everything documented: architecture
decisions, 105 SQL queries, ER diagrams, a data dictionary, and an interview
guide. Shall I walk you through the executive dashboard next?"
